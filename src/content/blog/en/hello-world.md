## Why "taimatsu"

「松明」 (taimatsu) means "torch" in Japanese. When wandering through the pitch-black forest of infrastructure, we want to be the torch lit first — a small light, but enough.

This site is our lab's homepage: black background, monospace type, terminal semantics — because that's the world we live in every day.

## What we're building

Four projects, one theme: **make complex things simple**.

- **novon.im** — a GitHub-based blogging platform. Import a repo, get a blog. Already live.
- **serverless.im** — a hub of practical serverless APIs.
- **infrastructure.im** — cloud infrastructure management; one-click deploy for services, apps and game servers.
- **containers.im** — an all-in-one container management tool, like Coolify.

Everything except novon is still under construction. Slowly, but moving.

## What this blog will cover

Build notes, post-mortems, release logs, and occasional rants about toolchains. All written in Markdown — after all, blogging platforms are our business.
